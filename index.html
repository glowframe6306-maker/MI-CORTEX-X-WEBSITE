Why do you use custom quotations?

Software projects differ in scope, integrations, design, platforms, security and delivery requirements. A custom quotation is more accurate and responsible than a fake fixed price.

Can a project be completed in stages?

Yes. Suitable projects can be divided into planned milestones so the most important features are delivered first.

Is maintenance included?

Maintenance can be included in the project proposal or arranged as a separate ongoing service.
